OPEN METHOD

How the Employee Reality Index works

Scoring rules, question wording, thresholds and limitations should be visible to everyone who reads the Index.

Important limitation

The Index describes experiences reported by self-selected respondents. It is not a representative estimate of all Indian IT workers, and it is not proof of wrongdoing by any company.

Monthly Pulse questions

The planned fixed survey contains ten questions. The wording below is intended to remain the same each month.

  1. How many rounds were in your most recent interview process?
  2. After a rejection, how often did you receive feedback?
  3. How long did you wait for an offer letter?
  4. Was your notice period respected by the new company?
  5. Did the new company demand a joining date earlier than your notice allowed?
  6. How did your last hike compare with your expectation?
  7. What was your last hike band?
  8. How often did you work unpaid beyond normal hours?
  9. How often was your leave cancelled or questioned?
  10. How secure does your job feel right now?

A rotating Support question about confidence in grievance handling is proposed as an additional question every second month.

Proposed score mapping

Each applicable answer maps to a score from 0 to 100. Better reported experiences score higher. “Not applicable” is excluded from the relevant mean.

Intermediate scores are planned as follows: rounds 3–4 = 75, 5–6 = 35; feedback Sometimes = 50; offer wait 3–7 = 80, 8–14 = 60, 15–30 = 30; Partly respected = 50; hike Met = 75 and Below = 25; hike bands 11–15 = 80, 6–10 = 60, 1–5 = 30; unpaid work Sometimes = 67 and Often = 33; leave Sometimes = 50; job security Somewhat = 67 and Not very = 33.

Sub-indices and weights

The six planned sub-indices are Hiring Reality, Notice and Joining, Pay and Hikes, Workload and Culture, Job Security, and Support. Each sub-index is the mean of its answered questions. The proposed overall score weights the first five sub-indices at 20% each. Support starts at 0% until there is enough volume and its weight can be reviewed.

Headline publication threshold: 30 valid responses per month. At 30–99, the score is labelled “Early estimate”; at 100+, that label is removed. Show month-over-month change and a 3–6 month trend only from published monthly aggregates.

Privacy and publication rules

  • No company name, name, email, phone number, exact age, gender or IP address in the Pulse.
  • Optional context uses bands only: company type, experience band, city, role family and work mode.
  • Breakdowns require at least five valid responses in each displayed group, with complementary suppression to prevent inference by subtraction.
  • Raw responses should never be available to the public browser. Public views should expose aggregates only.
  • Flagged responses are excluded from scoring and their exclusion reason is audited.
  • One response per device per month is intended, with Turnstile and abuse checks.
