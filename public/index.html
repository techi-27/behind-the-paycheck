THE CORPORATE CIRCUS

Indian IT: the plot twists write themselves.

We roast the systems, policies and corporate clichés—not individual employees. These are jokes, not claims about any specific company.

SATIRE MODE: ON
🎤THE INTERVIEW MARATHON

“One final round.” — Round 7

Technical round. Manager round. Director round. HR round. Astrology round. Still waiting for feedback like it’s a production deployment.

💸THE APPRAISAL FESTIVAL

Congratulations on your 3.7% happiness.

Inflation got promoted. Your rent got promoted. Your responsibilities got promoted. Your salary got a participation certificate.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦“WE ARE A FAMILY”

Family meeting. Bring your own laptop.

We’re a family until you ask about overtime, a raise, or why the “urgent” task arrived at 6:59 PM.

⏳NOTICE-PERIOD OLYMPICS

Your next job starts in 30 days. Your release says “maybe.”

New employer: “Can you join immediately?” Current employer: “Can you document the last 11 years first?”

📈CTC MAGIC

₹12 LPA CTC. ₹12 questions about take-home.

Fixed pay, variable pay, bonus, benefits, deductions. The offer letter is less a salary document and more an escape room.

🧑‍💻THE BENCH EXPERIENCE

No project. Still “utilization” is your KPI.

“Upskill yourself.” “Stay billable.” “Why are you not allocated?” Three managers, four dashboards, zero project context.

🚨PRODUCTION SUPPORT

It’s a P1. The runbook is a screenshot from 2019.

Everyone asks for an ETA. Nobody knows the dependency. Somehow the person who just joined is sharing the screen.

🏢RETURN TO OFFICE

Collaboration works best from your assigned chair.

Join the same calls, use the same chat, share the same screen—but now do it after commuting two hours to sit beside your headphones.
