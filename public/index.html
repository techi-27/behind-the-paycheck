EXPERIENCES THAT DESERVE TO BE HEARD

It is more than a payslip.

These are illustrative examples of the kinds of experiences this project is designed to document—not verified submissions.

HIRING & NOTICE PERIODS

“I can join in 15 days”—but my notice period is 90.

When one employer expects a full notice period and another wants an immediate joiner, candidates can feel stuck between two systems.

SALARY & RECOGNITION

A small hike after a year of extra effort.

Employees may be asked to take on more responsibility while seeing little change in their take-home pay.

INTERVIEW EXPERIENCE

Several rounds, then no answer.

Repeated interviews without clear feedback can leave candidates waiting without knowing where they stand.

WORKPLACE PRESSURE

Work ends on paper, not always in real life.

Late hours, urgent messages and unpaid extra work can spill into family time and rest.

KEEP THE PROJECT RUNNING

PRIVACY FIRST

Your story is not worth exposing your identity.

Do not include employee names, phone numbers, email addresses, personal identifiers or confidential company information. We aim to publish issue patterns, not expose individuals.

Current prototype reports stay in the browser where they are submitted. They are not yet shared across visitors or sent to a central database. Do not submit sensitive details.
