PRIVACY FIRST

Your story is not worth exposing your identity.

Do not include employee names, phone numbers, email addresses, personal identifiers or confidential company information. We aim to publish issue patterns, not expose individuals.

Current prototype reports stay in the browser where they are submitted. They are not yet shared across visitors or sent to a central database. Do not submit sensitive details.
